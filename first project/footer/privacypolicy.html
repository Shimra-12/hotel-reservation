Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2026

At Hotel, we value the trust our guests place in us and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have regarding your data.

1. Information We Collect

When you make a reservation, contact us, or use our website, we may collect information such as your name, email address, phone number, mailing address, payment details, and any preferences you share with us regarding your stay.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information collected to process reservations, respond to inquiries, improve our services, send booking confirmations, and, with your consent, share occasional offers or updates about the hotel.

3. Sharing of Information

We do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties. Information may be shared with trusted service providers, such as payment processors, solely to the extent necessary to fulfill your reservation or request.

4. Cookies

Our website may use cookies to enhance your browsing experience, remember your preferences, and understand how visitors use our site. You can disable cookies through your browser settings at any time.

5. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, loss, or misuse. However, no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to access, update, or request deletion of your personal information at any time. To do so, please contact us using the details provided on our Contact Us page.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page along with the date of the most recent update.

8. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your information is handled, please reach out to us via our Contact Us page.
