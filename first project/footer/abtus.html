About Us

Twenty years of hospitality, right by the sea

Our Story

What began over twenty years ago as a small beachside guesthouse has grown into one of the most loved retreats along the coast. Our founders started with a simple idea: give travelers a place that feels less like a hotel and more like a second home, right at the water's edge.

Over the decades, that idea grew room by room, guest by guest, into the hotel you see today — a favorite stop for tourists from all over the world who come for the beach and stay for the warmth of the people who run it.

Our Staff

None of this would mean anything without the people who welcome our guests every single day. Our staff are known for their warmth, patience, and genuine care — always ready with a smile, a helpful hand, or a local tip that makes a stay feel special.

From the moment you arrive to the moment you check out, they make sure you feel at home, comfortable, and truly looked after, just as they have for guests for the past twenty years.
