Events at Hotel

From beachside celebrations to seasonal gatherings, we love bringing our guests and community together throughout the year.

Past Events

Women's Day Celebration

Every March, we host a special evening honoring the women in our community and among our guests, with live music, a dedicated dinner menu, and small tokens of appreciation for every woman who joined us.

New Year Beach Party

Guests and locals gathered right on the sand to welcome the new year with music, fireworks, and a festive dinner under the stars, one of our most anticipated nights of the year.

Cultural Food Festival

A weekend celebrating local flavors, where our chefs and guest cooks came together to showcase traditional dishes, live cooking stations, and cultural performances for guests to enjoy.
